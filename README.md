zero_one chased after Rust, the latter's agility not even competing with zero_one's.

zero_one: "Fuck- Rust! Please, come back!"

They called out to him, almost tripping over it's own feet. They panted and paused in their chase, catching their breath. When they looked up, Rust was gone.

===

Rust sprints away from the crowd, making sure the figure of zero_one couldn't be seen. He stumbles towards wherever his legs are taking him, the lingering adrenaline from the protest suddenly tiring him out. Why now? He thought — he couldn't let Tesla, Armstrong- hell, even Doc know about his identity.

And especially not zero_one either.

Rust staggers towards a clearing in the crowd, swerving to make sure zero_one didn't keep track of his current location.

..Just how am I going to come back after that now? Rust thinks.

===

It stops in it's tracks, searching the area.

I really fucked up now. They thought. I can't do anything else.. I'm going to have to go to Tesla, he's the only person who has Rust's contact.

zero_one changes paths, heading back to it's shared house with guilt and sympathy in it's head. They arrive back home in a sweat, before walking inside and slamming the door to Tesla's room. Tesla jolted upright from whatever she was doing, throwing a wrench towards zero_one that was previously in her hand by instinct. Before Tesla can speak, zero_one speaks up.

That's not normal. Usually Zero is..

zero_one: "TESLA, Rust- rustismissingandithinkhemightvediedandswappedhosorsumthyisnekinesahiedsidkuhfeksdmBUT I don't think-"

They rambled on, panicking while it didn't seem to notice their words were slurring nonsensically. Tesla pinched the bridge of her nose, before interrupting the machine's rapid series of words.

Tesla: "Slow down, I can BARELY hear you!"

zero_one paused, apologizing under it's breath. Tesla crossed their arms across his chest, waiting for what zero_one has to say. Something about Rust? I swear if Rust died again we're gonna all be in so much trouble- Tesla thought.

zero_one:"Rust is MISSING!!!! You have to help me find him!"

Tesla froze. Rust was missing? Well, that wasn't really much of a surprise. After all, everyone had to change hosts quite often-

zero_one: "And- and I don't think he willingly changed hosts this time? He just ran off during a protest!"

Okay, now THAT was alarming. Normally, zero_one would keep a close eye on their best friend.

zero_one: "Tesla, please help me.. I- I need your phone for a sec. You have his contact, right????!!!!!!!!"

zero_one rapidly shook her shoulders while sweating bullets. It took quite some time for Tesla to process all of the machine's words. He sighed after realizing the reality of the whole situation, and what limited options she had.

Tesla: "..I do," She paused, slapping zero_one's arms off their shoulders.

Tesla: "But if you want it, don't fucking touch me."

They pulled away.

zero_one: "Right! Sorry.."

Tesla ambled off to a corner of his workshop before taking out their burner phone. She opened the contacts app, looking for Rust's display name. Before he could even click on it, zero_one had snatched the phone as fast as machine-ly possible and dashed out of the room, slamming the door open, mumbling an inaudible 'thanks'.

Tesla gave the now damaged hinges of the door a withering look.

===

zero_one made it out of the house, scrolling rapidly on Tesla's burner phone for Rust's contact. They clicked on it and their fingers moved across the phone, as they had never used mobile technology before. They eventually clicked on the display name and SOMEHOW managed to find his location. It pursued after the location in worried hopes to find their best friend. They didn't even care about the 'secret' in a dreadful situation like this.

Rust had been continuing around aimlessly while guilt and shame were practically parasites in his mind by now. I should've told them sooner, he kept thinking. It was as if the thoughts were torturing him.

Around the corner, a blur of orange and black could be seen in his peripheral vision.

..It couldn't b-

"RUST!" A high voice drew from around the corner. Please don't, the former thought. Then the blur disappeared, and in front of him was the same robot he had spent what felt like eternity running from.

zero_one, in the flesh.

Rust pulled away as he felt zero_one grab his wrist. He felt something- were robots even capable of crying?

zero_one: "Rust! Rust- I- I finally found you!" zero_one gazed at Rust with that sickening pitiful face full of concern.

zero_one: "You worried me so much, are you o-"

Rust shoved zero_one off of himself and took off, aimlessly trudging while trying his best to sprint away from zero_one.

zero_one: "Hey- RUST!" zero_one called from behind, their voice crackling with every step it took towards Rust.

Rust ended up in some sort of alleyway. He stopped there, catching his own breath while tears threatened to spill. He panted nervously, struggling to catch his breath. The adrenaline really was wearing off..



What should I do.. what should I do.. they're so stubborn, it's..



..Just what are they going to say about m-



zero_one: "--please, hear me out!!"

It really was his unlucky day, huh.

zero_one managed to catch up with Rust. Rust turned around, not even being able to meet the other's eyes.

zero_one: "I've.. been thinking, since that m-moment... If this is right to say or not, but-"

zero_one momentarily paused, trying to find the right words.

zero_one: "I really don't care about who you were. If you're comfortable in a host like this.. I'm okay with that, because I- no matter who or what you are or were, you'll always be my dearest friend!"

zero_one: "And that's.. that's because I want to keep being best friends with you, Rust. The real you you're comfortable being! None of my feelings will ever change- just, please, Rust. Understand that.."

zero_one practically shouted, hoping that Rust would get the words. The chartreuse robot in front of them tensed up, and zero_one could see that.

Rust: "..I get it, Zee- you don't have to repeat it. Let's just- part ways already." A hint of genuine distress could be clear in his tone.

Rust: "If that's the case.. then, you'll just end up being even more considerate. Like how you talk to me, you'd worry about it.."

Rust: "And probably waver over it, too. If that's the case, then- I don't think there's anything good with being with someone like me if it keeps paining you, anyway."

Rust's voice was unusually shaky for someone who was usually rebellious and extreme.

zero_one: "..!" zero_one gazed into Rust's eyes, it's expression befuddled before shifting into something more determined.

zero_one: "Th- that's not true! That's not what I want to talk about!"

Rust: "—then, what-"

zero_one: "If that's really what you believe, why aren't you running away like earlier..? Please, I don't want to stop being friends, really! Nobody else does- not Tesla, Doc or Armstrong, we all care about you! Please, Rust, can't you just listen? Everyone wants to keep being your friend! The real, real you who isn't what others think of him!"

zero_one: "Because— you're so, so, so so precious, Rust! You wouldn't understand!"

zero_one shouted, their voice crackling with raw emotion that was surprising coming from a tombsona.
